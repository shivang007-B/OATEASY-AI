verified_user OatEasy AI OS — Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Effective Date: January 2026 · Compliant with ISO/IEC 27001, GDPR & Commercial Culinary Standards

security 1. Commercial Kitchen Data Confidentiality

OatEasy AI operates on strict enterprise privacy principles. Your restaurant's proprietary recipes, sales covers, ingredient unit costs, vendor pricing negotiations, and dish profit margins are strictly confidential and encrypted with military-grade AES-256.

model_training 2. Machine Learning Telemetry & Isolation

Neural demand forecasting curves and perishable expiry risk algorithms are containerized per organization. OatEasy AI never shares, pools, or exposes your operational data to competitor restaurants, third-party brokers, or external advertising networks.

sync_lock 3. POS & Inventory Integration Security

When you connect Point-of-Sale (POS) systems, kitchen display units (KDS), or digital weighing scales, communications are secured through TLS 1.3 encrypted tunnels with automated API token rotation and zero credential exposure.

shield_person 4. Staff & Operator Access Governance

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) ensures Executive Chefs, Storekeepers, and General Managers only access workflows relevant to their duty stations. Every purchase order approval, inventory audit, and waste write-off is logged in an immutable audit trail.

delete_forever 5. Data Ownership & 1-Click Export

You retain 100% intellectual property ownership of your culinary and sales data. You may download complete historical CSV/JSON backups or request complete data deletion at any time via your account dashboard.
